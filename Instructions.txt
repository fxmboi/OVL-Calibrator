================================================================================
  📡 OVL CALIBRATOR - Vive/Index Base Station 2D Alignment Utility (v1.0)
================================================================================

Welcome to the Overlay Visual Link (OVL) Calibrator! This tool connects directly 
to SteamVR tracking data and flattens your room space into a technical 2D map, 
helping you find the exact tracking angles and remove controller blind spots.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🚀 QUICK START GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Turn on your HTC Vive or Valve Index physical base stations.
2. Launch SteamVR on your computer and ensure your hardware is fully green/awake.
3. Double-click 'ovl_calibrator.exe'. 
4. The tool will instantly sync to your tracking space coordinates and render
   your room architecture layout dynamically.

*NOTE: If SteamVR is closed or your hardware is sleeping, the application will
 display a vibrant orange "WAITING FOR STEAMVR..." banner and clear the map grid.
 Once your devices wake up, the map updates automatically!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
📐 DIAGNOSTIC METRICS: HOW TO READ THE DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• THE BLUEPRINT FLOOR GRID: 
  Each grid block represents exactly 1 SQUARE METER in real life. Use this to
  visually gauge the size and dimensions of your active VR tracking space.

• ACTIVE BASE STATIONS:
  Station B is ALWAYS assigned to the device furthest to your left.
  Station C is ALWAYS assigned to the device furthest to your right.

• DISTANCE BETWEEN UNITS:
  Measures the direct physical line-of-sight tracking footprint between the 
  two base stations in meters. (Max recommended: 5m for v1.0, 7m for v2.0).

• SIGHTLINE DEVIATION:
  This is your error margin. It tracks how far left or right a base station 
  is swiveled away from looking dead-center at the opposite device.
  - 0.0° Deviation means it is pointing perfectly at the opposite unit.
  - Try to adjust your physical swivels to get these numbers as low as possible.

• WALL ANGLES:
  Displays the intuitive, clean inward angle (from 0° to 90°) of your base 
  station's hardware lens relative to your physical corner walls.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🎯 TARGET CALIBRATION FOOTPRINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Each base station projects a translucent wedge and a thin center laser sight.
- Physically swivel your base stations left or right until the laser sight lines 
  intersect directly into the center of the opposite green tracker box icon.
- When your alignment hits the optimal 60-degree overlapping cone limit, the 
  status alert banner transforms to a solid green "ALIGNMENT OK"!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
🔒 SAFETY & POWER AUTO-CLOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
You don't need to manually close this app when you are done. The software features 
live power cycle hooks. The moment you turn off your VR hardware or close out 
SteamVR, the tool will automatically terminate and shut down cleanly on its own.
